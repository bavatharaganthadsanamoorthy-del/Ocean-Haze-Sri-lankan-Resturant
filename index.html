Our story

A Sri Lankan Gem
on the Welsh Coast

🌊

Coastal Setting

Nestled just outside St Davids — the UK's smallest city — Ocean Haze is a hidden gem combining the serene beauty of Pembrokeshire with warm Sri Lankan hospitality.

🍛

Authentic Flavours

Every dish uses traditional Sri Lankan techniques. Curries are slow-cooked with hand-ground spices, parathas made fresh each evening, sambols crafted from scratch daily.

🌿

Fresh & Inclusive

Wide selection of vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options. Every ingredient is sourced carefully to ensure quality, freshness and authenticity in every bite.

🎉

Friday Buffet Night

Every Friday an extraordinary spread — amazing variety of rice and curry dishes, perfect for first-timers and seasoned fans alike. Remarkable value and absolutely delicious.
